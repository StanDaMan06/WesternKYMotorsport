WKY MotorSports
RACING UNIVERSE
EARLY ACCESS · V0.5
THREE CAREERS. ONE PADDOCK.

YOUR RACE.
YOUR LEGACY.

Lead a championship-winning constructor. Command the pit wall. Or fight your way from the junior categories to Formula 1. Three standalone simulation modes with local saves and career backups.

🏆
MANAGEMENT

Team Principal

Run a real-world constructor. Develop the car, sign drivers, simulate 24 races and chase the Drivers' and Constructors' titles.

🎧
STRATEGY

Race Engineer

Choose from all 24 calendar circuits and manage full-distance Grands Prix from a live race display. Control tyres, driver pace, ERS and pit stops.

🏎️
CAREER

Driver Career

Begin in Formula 3, earn your way to F2 and fight for a seat in Formula 1 with performance-based offers.
